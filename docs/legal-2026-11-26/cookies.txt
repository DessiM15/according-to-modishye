## 1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

### 1.1 Defining a Cookie:

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer, smartphone, tablet, or other device when you visit a website. Cookies allow a website to recognize your browser or device, remember certain information about your visit, and support website functionality.

Cookies may be temporary, meaning they are deleted when you close your browser (session cookies), or persistent, meaning they remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them.

### 1.2 Cookies on MODISHYE's Platform:

a) MODISHYE may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality, maintain security, remember visitor preferences, and, where applicable, collect statistical information about website usage and visitor interactions.

b) Depending on the website configuration and the services integrated into the Website, cookies may support features such as session continuity, website navigation, remembering preferences, and processing transactions.

c) The specific cookies used may vary depending on the Website's functionality, third-party integrations, and the services available at the time of your visit.

### 1.3 Functionalities of Cookies:

a) Cookies and similar technologies may operate automatically when you visit the Website, depending on their purpose and the applicable consent requirements.

b) Cookies may contain identifiers or other information that distinguishes your browser or device. Although some cookies do not directly identify you by name, information collected through cookies may be linked with other information and may constitute personal data.

c) You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings and, where available, through the Website's cookie preference controls. However, disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

d) MODISHYE will seek consent before using cookies or similar technologies where consent is legally required. Strictly necessary cookies may be used without consent where the applicable legal exemption permits their use.

### 1.4 Utilizing Different Cookies:

a) Cookies established by the owner of the Website, in this instance MODISHYE, are categorized as "first-party cookies." Cookies established by entities other than the Website owner are designated as "third-party cookies."

b) Third-party cookies may be used by external service providers to provide features or functionalities on or through the Website, such as analytics, embedded content, social media features, or advertising, where those services are integrated into the Website.

c) Third-party providers may collect information about your device and browsing activity in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies. MODISHYE does not control the independent cookie practices of third-party providers and encourages users to review the applicable third-party policies.

Various types of cookies and similar technologies may include:

**(i) Strictly Necessary Cookies:**

These cookies are essential for the operation, security, and basic functionality of the Website. They may support website navigation, security features, session management, and other functions specifically requested by the visitor. Where these cookies are strictly necessary to provide the requested online service, consent may not be required under applicable law.

**(ii) Advertising Cookies:**

Where advertising services or advertising technologies are integrated into the Website, advertising cookies may be used to collect information about users' browsing activities, viewed content, followed links, browser, device, or IP address. Such information may be used to measure advertising effectiveness or deliver advertisements based on browsing activity.

Advertising cookies are not treated as strictly necessary. Where required by applicable law, MODISHYE will obtain consent before placing or accessing advertising cookies or using similar tracking technologies for these purposes.

**(iii) First-Party Analytics Cookies:**

Where analytics tools are integrated into the Website, first-party analytics cookies may help measure website traffic, understand visitor interactions, identify popular pages, and evaluate website performance. The information may be used to improve the Website's content, layout, and functionality.

Whether analytics cookies require consent depends on the applicable law and the nature and configuration of the technology. MODISHYE will obtain consent where required.

**(iv) Third-Party Analytics Cookies:**

MODISHYE may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, if integrated into the Website. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visitor interactions, website performance, and traffic patterns.

The use of any specific analytics provider will depend on the Website's actual configuration. Where third-party analytics cookies require consent, they will only be activated after the required consent has been obtained.

**(v) Social and Content Cookies:**

Where social media plugins, embedded content, videos, or other third-party content are integrated into the Website, those services may use cookies or similar technologies to support their functionality and collect information about visitor interactions.

Some third-party services may also use collected information for analytics, advertising, or market research, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable law. Where consent is required, these technologies will not be activated before the required consent is obtained.
