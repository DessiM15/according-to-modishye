# How to publish an article

Your portal: https://www.accordingtomodishyeblog.com/studio (or the old /wp-admin address, both go to the same place). Log in with your Google account. It works on a laptop or a phone.

## Write a new article

1. Click **Articles** on the left, then the **+** at the top.
2. The post opens already laid out: an intro heading, three sections and a closing section. Replace the placeholder text with yours. Delete a section you do not need, or copy one to add more.
3. Fill in the four boxes above the text:
   - **Title**. Say what the reader gets. Under 70 characters shows in full on Google.
   - **Shelf**. Pick the chapter it belongs to.
   - **Cover image**. Drag a photo in, or click to upload. Then write one line describing it.
   - **YouTube video**. Only if there is a matching video. Paste the link.
4. To add an image inside the text, click the **+** that appears between paragraphs and choose Image. Describe it in the box underneath.
5. To add a link, select the words and press the link button, then paste the address.
6. Click **Preview on site** at the bottom to see it on the real website in a new tab. Nobody else can see it yet.
7. Click **Publish**. It is live within a minute.

Everything is saved as you type. Close the tab and come back any time, the draft is where you left it.

## The Google tab

The portal writes the search description for you from your first paragraph. If you want a different one, open the **Google** tab at the top of the post and write one or two sentences in **Search description**. Keep it under 155 characters, the counter turns orange when you go over.

Do not change the **Web address** of a post that is already published. Links people have shared would stop working.

## Edit a published article

Open it from **Articles**, change what you want, click **Publish** again. Use **Preview on site** first if you want to check it.

## Take an article down

Open it, click the three dots next to Publish, choose **Unpublish**. It leaves the site and stays in the portal as a draft. **Delete** removes it for good.

## Guides and legal pages

**Guides (shop)** holds the text and cover for each PDF. The price shown on the site is on the Shop tab, keep it the same as the price in Shopify, which is where people actually pay. **Legal pages** are the privacy policy, terms, refunds and cookies pages.
