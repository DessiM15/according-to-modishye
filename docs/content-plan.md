# Content plan for search

Her stated focus is the high school to college transition, college applications, finances and financial aid. Today the library has 3 articles on getting in and 1 on money, out of 25. The site structure is built for that focus. These articles fill it. Each title is a real search phrase. Write them in her voice, from her own road, 900 to 1500 words, one per week.

## Rules that keep the search value

- One article, one question, answered in the first paragraph.
- Her story in the body, not the title.
- Link each article to its shelf, one guide and one older article.
- Add a short FAQ at the end of the money articles. Those get rich results.
- Keep the old URLs. New articles get short slugs like `/articles/fafsa-first-time`.
