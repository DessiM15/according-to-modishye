# Privacy Policy update for the new site (draft)

*This is a draft prepared by your web developer to match the policy to how the new site works. It is not legal advice. Please read it, answer the questions at the end, and have a lawyer look it over if you want certainty.*

Nothing in this file is live. Once you approve the wording, it goes into the portal under **Legal pages > Privacy Policy**.

---

## 1. What is changing and why

Your current policy (effective February 20, 2024) was written for the old WordPress site. The new site handles people's information differently in a few places, so the policy needs to say so. Most of your policy stays exactly as it is.

**What the new site does with people's information**

| Where | What is collected | Who handles it |
|---|---|---|
| Newsletter sign-up | Email address and the stage the person picks (applying, first year, graduate, parent, or reader). They must click a confirmation email before they are added. | Mailchimp |
| Contact form | Name, email, topic, subject and message. They tick a box agreeing to the privacy policy. | Web3Forms delivers it to your support inbox |
| Speaking requests (same form) | The above, plus school or organisation, audience type, audience size, in person or virtual, city, and date. | Web3Forms delivers it to your support inbox |
| Buying a guide | Handled on your existing WooCommerce store, which moves to its own "shop" address. Card payments go through WooPayments. | WooCommerce on Bluehost, WooPayments |
| Videos | Videos are YouTube embeds in "privacy-enhanced" mode. | YouTube (Google) |
| The site itself | Standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages requested). | Vercel hosts the site, Sanity stores the articles and images |

**What the new site does not do**

- It has no analytics or visitor tracking installed (no Google Analytics, no advertising pixels).
- It sets no cookies for ordinary visitors. Cookies are only used on the shop address, where WooCommerce needs them to hold the order and the buyer's login.
- It has no social media login, no marketplace, and no advertising.

Your current policy and cookies page say the site uses analytics tools, advertising cookies and social login. On the new site those statements are no longer accurate, which is the main reason for this update.

**The changes at a glance**

1. New effective date.
2. Newsletter section rewritten to describe Mailchimp, the stage question and the confirmation email.
3. New section for the contact form and speaking requests.
4. Payment section updated to name WooPayments and the separate shop address.
5. New section for embedded YouTube videos.
6. New list of the companies that process data for the site.
7. Cookie and analytics statements corrected.
8. Retention section gains two lines, for the newsletter and for messages.

## 3. The Cookies page needs a matching change

Your Cookies page says the site uses advertising cookies, Google Analytics and social media cookies, and it refers to a table of cookies that is empty. None of that is true of the new site. Suggested replacement for the section "What Kind of Cookies do We Use?":

> **What Kind of Cookies do We Use?**
>
> The main Website does not set cookies for ordinary visitors. It stores one small note in your browser for the length of your visit, so that the opening animation only plays once. This note holds no personal information and is removed when you close the tab.
>
> Our shop pages use strictly necessary cookies set by WooCommerce. These remember what is in your order and, if you have an account, keep you signed in. Payment pages may also use cookies set by our payment processor to prevent fraud.
>
> Embedded YouTube videos may set cookies from YouTube when a video loads or plays.
>
> We do not use advertising cookies.

If you go with this, the paragraphs describing advertising cookies, analytics cookies, and "reporting and analytics cookies" (sections 1.4 (ii) to (v) and 4.1) should be removed or reworded. I can draft that once you answer question 1.

## 4. Questions only you can answer

1. **Analytics.** The new site has no visitor tracking. Do you want it to stay that way (Option A in 2.6), or do you want a tool such as Google Analytics added? If added, the policy and Cookies page must name it.
2. **Marketplace and partners.** The policy mentions a "MODISHYE Marketplace" where third parties offer products, and "collaborators and partners". Do you have, or plan, anything like that? If not, those passages could be removed.
3. **Identity checks.** The policy says you may collect "identity documentation", "photo ID", "bank details" and run "background checks". Do you do any of this? The new site collects none of it.
4. **Apple Pay and Google Pay.** Are these switched on in your WooCommerce checkout? If not, section 2.5 b) can go.
5. **Billing and shipping addresses.** Your guides are PDFs. Does your checkout ask for a full billing address, and is "shipping addresses" in section 2.1 still needed?
6. **Younger readers.** Section 9.6 says the site is "not directed at children". The newsletter has an option for students applying to college, some of whom will be under 18. Are you comfortable with that wording, or should a lawyer review it?
7. **Visitors outside the United States.** Section 6.2 says you will "seek your consent" before sending data outside the European Economic Area. Every provider listed above is in the United States or stores data there, and the site does not ask for a separate consent. Do you have readers or buyers in Europe or the UK? If so, this section is worth a lawyer's review.
8. **Business details.** Is "MODISHYE" the correct legal name for the Data Controller, and should the policy give a postal address or state?
9. **Launch date.** The date to put in "Last updated".
